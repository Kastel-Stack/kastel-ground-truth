# Core Philosophy

Kastel is an open, human-supervised adaptive scaling OS for sovereign, digitally legible enterprises.

It treats business growth as a supervised learning loop rather than an automation race.

Scaling does not mean maximising growth, automation, engagement, extraction or output at any cost. It means widening the viable operating band of the enterprise while preserving truth, judgement, source-of-truth discipline, privacy, solvency and coupling to a real niche.

In Trident-G terms, Kastel treats business scaling as an allostatic problem. The system should help the enterprise stay inside a viable operating band as load, uncertainty and opportunity change.

The operating aim is governed adaptation: diagnose state-niche fit, act when the next move can teach, clarify when the niche is opaque, and bank only patterns that remain portable, bounded and safe.
