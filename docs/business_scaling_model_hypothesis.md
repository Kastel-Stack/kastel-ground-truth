# Business Scaling Model Hypothesis

Every Kastel node should state the scaling model it is testing before it proposes optimisation or automation.

The question is not "How do we maximise this business?" It is "What scaling model are we testing, and is the business in enough state-niche fit for the next action to teach?"

A Business Scaling Model Hypothesis should describe how offer, niche, proof, route to market, delivery, feedback and governance may form a controlled learning loop. It should also state the viable operating band: what load, risk, workload, automation, trust and evidence limits must remain bounded while the model is tested.

Kastel treats scaling as allostatic capability-building, not extraction. A stronger scaling model widens what the enterprise can safely handle while remaining truthful, human-governed, source-of-truth grounded and coupled to its real niche.
